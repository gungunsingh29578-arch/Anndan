Donation Agreement and Terms

1. I confirm that I am authorized to donate the listed surplus food and that the information submitted regarding food item, quantity, timing, and condition is accurate to the best of my knowledge.

2. The food has been prepared, stored, and handled in a hygienic manner and is safe for donation at the time of handover, subject to inspection by the receiver.

3. I agree to disclose any known allergy, contamination, storage, or safety risk associated with the food before pickup or handover.

4. The receiving organisation may inspect, refuse, or reject the donation if the food is unsafe, mislabeled, expired, improperly packaged, or non-compliant with local food safety standards.

5. I understand that submission does not guarantee pickup, acceptance, or legal clearance, and that Annadan acts only as a coordination platform and not as a government authority, food regulator, or licensed food safety inspector.
