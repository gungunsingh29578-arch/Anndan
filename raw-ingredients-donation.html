Donation Agreement and Terms

1. I confirm that I am legally authorized to donate the listed raw ingredients and that the declared quantity, type, and condition are accurate.

2. The ingredients are clean, hygienic, properly packed, and safe for human or animal consumption at the time of donation, subject to inspection by the receiving organisation.

3. I will disclose any known food safety, allergy, storage, or handling concerns before handover and acknowledge that false or unsafe information may result in rejection of the donation.

4. The selected NGO or receiver may inspect, accept, refuse, or reschedule the collection based on food safety, packaging, quality, or compliance requirements.

5. I understand that submission does not guarantee pickup or acceptance, and that Annadan acts only as a coordination platform and is not a food safety authority or government body.
