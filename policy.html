Donation Policy & Terms

1. Purpose

Annadan is a food donation coordination platform created to connect donors with verified receivers for safe and responsible redistribution of surplus food and essentials. The platform supports community welfare and helps reduce food waste.

2. Donor responsibility

Donors must ensure they are legally authorized to donate the food items or ingredients listed, that all submitted information is true and complete, and that the donation is safe, hygienic, and suitable for the intended use.

3. Food safety and compliance

All food, ingredients, and prepared items shared through the platform must comply with applicable local food safety rules, hygiene norms, handling practices, and public health requirements. Any food that is unsafe, expired, contaminated, mislabeled, or improperly stored may be refused by the receiving organisation.

4. Acceptance and refusal

Submission of a donation does not guarantee acceptance, pickup, or delivery. The receiving organisation may inspect the item, reject it, or request additional information before collection.

5. Risk and liability

Users are responsible for the accuracy of their submissions and any legal, health, or safety issues arising from false, misleading, unauthorized, or unsafe donation details. Annadan acts only as a coordination service and is not a government authority, regulator, or food safety inspector.

6. Consent

By submitting donation information, the user confirms they have read and understood these terms and agree to comply with them. This consent applies to food donation, raw ingredients donation, and related community welfare activities facilitated by the platform.
